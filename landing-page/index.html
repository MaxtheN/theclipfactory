A few things worth knowing

Before you
press play.

No black box. No fine-print surprises.

Can I download the Mac app today?

The reproducible way to try Growth Factory is to run it from source on an Apple Silicon Mac. A verified, notarized public download is not available yet. The setup guide covers Python, Node, FFmpeg, and model installation.

Does my footage leave my Mac?

Local processing is the default. If you configure cloud model roles, their inputs go to the selected provider. Sharing still frames is opt-in. Publishing uploads the media you approve. You choose which optional services to connect.

Will it post without asking me?

No. You review and approve the exact version before publication. The Instagram flow requires a connected account and setup; the first real publication through the current app still needs live verification. Scheduling requires the app to stay open.

Are these real clips and performance results?

No. This walkthrough uses crops of one AI-generated alpine image, illustrative clip titles, and synthetic metrics. They explain the workflow, not customer results or a guarantee of engagement.

What does it cost to try?

The project’s source code is available under the MIT license. Local models need compatible hardware, storage, and their own license review. Optional cloud models and connected services may have separate costs.
