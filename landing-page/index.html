answered.

More in the user guide. Anything else: jamesbodebiyi@gmail.com.

What does it actually make?

Short vertical clips (9:16) from your long videos: podcast episodes, streams, talks. Agents find the moments, write the hook, cut, frame and caption them, and check each clip against your Style rules before you see it.

Which computers does it run on?

Mac, Windows and Linux. Local speech runs on Apple Silicon Macs and x64 Windows or Linux; other computers use cloud speech or an imported transcript. Signed installers come with the first signed release; until then, it installs from source.

Does it post without asking me?

No. Only a clip you approved can be exported or posted. Scheduled posts go out only while the app is open, and a time missed while it was closed waits for you instead of posting late.

Do I need to pay for AI?

No. Local models are free to run on a capable computer. Cloud models are optional: add your own OpenRouter or Groq key and pay the provider directly.

What happens if I cancel?

Your plan stays until the end of the period you paid for. Nothing is ever deleted: brands, clips and results stay, and only creating more than the plan allows is blocked.

Can I trust the numbers?

Results are real platform numbers. The Strategist calls its findings observations, shows the evidence, and never changes your Style rules without your approval.
