A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING.

Good questions.

A little clarity before the next creative leap.

What is Growth Factory?

A local-first creative workspace for turning source footage into short-form video. It brings campaign planning, creative direction, production, human review, and learning into one loop, with AI roles helping along the way.

Can I try it right now?

Yes — you can explore the interactive workspace demo. It uses illustrative sample data so you can get a feel for the workflow. The native Mac app is still in development; the hosted demo does not run models, render video, or publish content.

Does my footage leave my Mac?

The native workflow is designed to keep your source footage and model processing on your machine. External services, such as Instagram, require separate connection and authorization. This website and the hosted demo do not connect to your local media library.

Does it publish content automatically?

No. AI critique and human approval are separate steps. You review the creative direction and final output. Connecting an external account and publishing require their own deliberate authorization.

What Mac do I need?

Growth Factory is being developed for Apple Silicon Macs. Model availability and performance depend on your hardware, memory, and selected model. Final system requirements and a public release date have not been announced.
