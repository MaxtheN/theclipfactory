# Posting

Posting from the app needs the **Pro** or **Business** plan. On Free, export approved clips and post them yourself.

## Connecting accounts

In **Settings → Accounts and connections**, connect TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter) or Threads. Each one opens the platform's own sign-in page in your browser. Growth Factory never sees your password. Accounts are connected per brand: a brand only ever posts to the accounts you connected to it. Pro allows 3 connected accounts, Business 30.

## Posting a clip

Only an approved clip can be posted. From the clip, choose the accounts, check or edit the caption for each platform (the app can draft one), and choose **Post now** or **Schedule**. TikTok also asks for its privacy level and consent each time, as TikTok requires.

Posts go through Growth Factory's posting service and its posting partner, Zernio. The clip file and caption are sent for that post only.

## Schedules

Scheduled posts are sent by the app **while it is open** on your computer. Keep it open, and the computer awake, around the times you schedule. A schedule may still go out up to two minutes late (for example, after a short sleep).

## Missed schedules

If a scheduled time passes while the app was closed, or the computer was asleep for longer than that, the post is **never sent late on its own**. It becomes **overdue** and waits in **Activity** for you to choose:

- **Post now**: send it now;
- **Reschedule**: pick a new time;
- **Cancel**: drop it. The clip stays approved.

This also applies when posting was held by your plan (for example, after a downgrade left more connected accounts than the plan allows). Once the plan allows posting again, the hold is lifted. A time that passed during the hold is marked "This time passed while posting was held by the plan. Post now, reschedule or cancel."

## Recovery notices

When something needs you, the app says what happened and what to do:

- **Account needs reconnecting:** the platform ended the connection. Reconnect it in Accounts and connections.
- **Rate limited:** the platform or posting service asked us to slow down. The app shows when to try again.
- **Posting limit reached:** a limit on the shared posting service, not something you can fix. Contact support.
- **Posting unavailable:** the service is down or unreachable. Try again later; nothing was posted twice.
- **Plan limit:** posting or another account needs a higher plan. The app shows an upgrade prompt.

The app never retries a post in a way that could publish it twice.

## After posting

Posts made from the app are tracked in [Results](results.md) automatically and measured at 3 and 6 days.
