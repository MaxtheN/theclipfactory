# Results and Strategist

**Results** shows how your posted clips performed and what the factory learned. Everything here is a proposal for you to approve; nothing in Results posts, approves or spends.

## Tracked posts

Posts made from the app are tracked automatically against the exact approved clip and connected account. Each post is measured at **3 days** and **6 days**. Results compares the latest numbers with **your typical post**: this account's median for each metric. When there is no baseline yet, it says so rather than inventing one.

### Adding a result by hand

For posts made outside the app, use **Track a post → Add a result by hand**. You must choose the approved clip it matches, give the post URL or publication ID, the measurement window (when the numbers were read) and **Source, window & limits**: where the numbers came from and anything that limits them (for example "read from the Instagram app, 4 days after posting, reach not available"). A hand-entered result without its source, window and limits is not useful evidence, so the form requires them.

## Strategist

The **Strategist** reads this account's own measured posts and keeps a ledger of **beliefs** about what tends to beat your typical post, in three areas:

- **Craft:** hooks, length, captions, opening treatment;
- **Clip picking:** which kinds of moments to choose;
- **Timing:** day and hour of posting, in your computer's time zone.

Each belief shows the posts it is based on, its confidence and its status. A pattern must appear in at least two posts before it is even named. The Strategist then plans the next batch (mostly what is working, about a quarter exploring something new) and writes **proposals**: a Style rules change, clip-picking guidance, or a campaign change. Nothing changes until you approve the proposal on its screen.

## Factory upgrades

The factory notices editing styles it cannot render yet, such as a progress bar, a speaker name lower third or an animated subtitle box. It finds them in your references, critic findings, "Request a change" reasons, feedback notes and Strategist beliefs. Choose **Look for gaps** to scan. Each gap becomes a **Factory upgrade** with the quotes that triggered it. **Write work order** turns it into a coding task; **Not now** dismisses it.

Only when you click **Build it** does a builder agent write the code change, on its own branch, with tests, the exact commands it ran and a sample render, for a developer to review. Nothing is merged or installed automatically. Building needs a copy of the app that is a Git checkout (a developer setup); the installed app can find gaps and write work orders, but cannot build branches. Audio additions (music beds, sound effects) and split screens are listed but cannot be built yet. Building upgrades is part of the Business plan.

## What results can and cannot prove

Read the numbers as observations about your own account, not as proof.

- **Observational, not causal.** A clip that beat your typical post may have done so for reasons the factory cannot see (the news that day, the algorithm, who shared it). There is no statistical evaluation service behind Results; a belief's confidence describes how consistent the pattern has been, not that it caused the lift.
- **Small samples.** A few posts can move the median a lot. Treat early beliefs as ideas to test.
- **Hand-entered results** count only as far as their recorded source, window and limits allow.
- **Productivity claims** ("saves N hours a week") need a matched baseline: the same kind of footage, done the old way, timed, with receipts. Processing time shown in the app excludes queue time and your review time, and direct render cost is not the fully loaded cost per usable clip (see [Clip evidence](library.md#clip-evidence)).

The factory never claims a success rate or ROI for you. If you want to report one, measure it yourself against a matched baseline.
